Who we're for

Owners and operators building something they intend to keep for a long time.

Good design is quiet. It earns attention slowly.

Marrowfield is a studio of architects and interior designers who believe a building is only finished once people have lived in it for a while.

We start every project by understanding how a space will actually be used — the morning light, the way people move through a room, the wear a floor takes over ten years. Then we design backward from there.

The result is work that doesn't chase trends: rooms that feel calm on day one and better on day one thousand.

The studio

A small practice, by choice.

We keep the studio deliberately small so that the people you meet at the first sketch are the same people on site at the end. No hand-offs, no house style pushed onto every brief.

Founded in 2016, Marrowfield works across private homes, workspaces, and hospitality, with a growing body of work led by a team that would rather do a few things properly than many things quickly.
