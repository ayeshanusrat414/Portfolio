In the atelier

Made by hand, made to last.

Every Ashmere piece begins as a flat pattern on our cutting table and is sewn, start to finish, by a single maker. Nothing is rushed and nothing is mass-produced.

We work with deadstock and natural fibres wherever we can, and we keep our runs small on purpose — so each garment gets the attention it deserves, and so little goes to waste.

For made-to-measure, we take your measurements in the studio and fit the garment to you across two appointments.

— The Ashmere atelier
