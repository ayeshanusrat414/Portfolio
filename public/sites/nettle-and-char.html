The room

A table set for the season.

We change the menu when the market does — some weeks that means the first stone fruit, other weeks the last of the artichokes.

Nettle & Char began as a supper club of eight seats and a single wood oven. It has grown, but the idea hasn't: cook a few things properly, let the produce lead, and give people a reason to stay an hour longer than they planned.

Everything on the pass is made in-house, from the sourdough we bake each morning to the bitters behind the bar.

— Elena & Marco, owners
