A tour of the neighbourhood

Eight worlds, one afternoon.

Our exhibit floor takes you planet by planet — scaled models, real meteorites you can touch, and a few facts that never stop being strange.

Mars

The red planet

Home to the tallest volcano in the solar system — three times the height of Everest.

Jupiter

The giant

So large that all the other planets could fit inside it — with room to spare.

Saturn

The ringed one

Its rings are mostly ice, yet only about ten metres thick in places.

Neptune

The far blue

Winds here scream past at over 2,000 km/h — the fastest in the solar system.
